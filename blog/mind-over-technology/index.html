[ ../ ](/) # Mind over technology The mind is a first-class citizen. As someone who values the absence of technology but at the same time also has technology as a hobby, it can be easy to forget that the most important computer is not the metal box sitting on my desk, but the brain inside my head. All those hours spent on tinkering and tweaking my computer to enhance my workflow, make it operate, feel and look like the way I would like it to, I should also equally put if not much more time and energy into improving my mind.
