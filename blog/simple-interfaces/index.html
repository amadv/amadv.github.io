[ ../ ](/) # Simple interfaces I enjoy simple user interfaces. - \* Small - \* One dimensional - \* One style - \* One font - \* Monotone - \* Static (no motion) - \* Space for visual hierarchy - \* Minimal cognitive load Hence my love for [~~motherfuckingwebsite.com~~](https://motherfuckingwebsite.com) text user interfaces. Communicating only the necessary information in it's most naked form, text. This is why the terminal is my preferred choice of interface to my personal computer. This is also why my blog is my preferred choice of interface to connect with the world. Less noise, less visual clutter.
